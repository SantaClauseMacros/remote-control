    Want to lock everyone out?

    Devices connect with your PC ID, so clearing this list doesn't block anyone. To get a brand-new PC ID:

    1. Click Exit Remote Control below.
    2. Delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\RemoteControl.
    3. Start Remote Control again from the Start menu, and add the new ID on your own devices.

    Help

    Everything you need to get connected and playing.

    Connect from your phone

    1. On Home, scan the QR code with your phone's camera — or click Copy phone link and send it to yourself.
    2. Open the link, name the PC and tap Save, then Connect.
    3. Tip: Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.

    Touch controls

    • Drag to move the pointer · tap to click
    • Hold, then drag to click and drag
    • Two-finger tap to right-click · two-finger drag to scroll
    • Pinch to zoom · ⋯ for the keyboard and more keys

    🎮 Minecraft mode

    • Tap 🕹 in the top-left of the phone app
    • Tap anywhere to hit, hold to mine, drag to look
    • Tap a slot on Minecraft's hotbar to switch items
    • Inv is left of the hotbar, Drop is right of it
    • Sprint (Shift) and Crouch (Ctrl) toggle on and off

    Fixes for common problems

    • Can't click Task Manager or admin prompts? Use Restart as Administrator in Settings → Tools.
    • Laggy? Lower the quality on the device (⋯ → Quality → Low). Wired internet on the PC helps most.
    • Slow encoder warning? Close Parsec, OBS or ShadowPlay — they can take over the graphics card's video encoder.
    • Black screen? Windows blocks capture on the lock screen — sign in on the PC.
    Still stuck? Read the full guide or report a problem on GitHub.
