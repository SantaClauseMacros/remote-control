    Phone mic not working in Discord?

    1) Update the PC app. 2) In the PC app press "Install virtual microphone". 3) Restart Discord and pick "CABLE Output" as its mic. 4) Reconnect and tap Mic. "Check my setup" tells you which step is missing.

    Keyboard did not pop up?

    On iPhone tap the "Tap to type" button that appears when the PC is in a text box.

    Minecraft controls

    • Tap anywhere — hit · hold — mine
    • Drag — look around
    • Hotbar — tap the slot right on the game's own hotbar
    • Inv left of the hotbar · Drop right of it
    • Use or two-finger tap — place / eat / use
    • Joystick — walk · Jump · Sprint (Shift) and Crouch (Ctrl) tap on / off
    • Esc, Chat, View (F5), Swap (F) — top-left
    • In your inventory and menus, tap exactly where you want to click; hold to drag an item
    • 🕹 — show / hide controls · ⋯ — keyboard, quality, leave

    If the hotbar targets don't line up, match this to Minecraft's GUI Scale setting.
